# Problem Statement

## Background

Medical research evolves continuously.

Scientific publications appear every day.

Clinical evidence changes over time.

Patients struggle to maintain an up-to-date understanding of their condition.

---

## Current Problems

- Information is fragmented.
- Papers are difficult to interpret.
- Medical news often exaggerates findings.
- Scientific consensus changes.
- Valuable information is easily forgotten.
- Personal relevance is rarely addressed.

---

## Desired Outcome

A continuously evolving evidence-based knowledge system capable of translating scientific research into trustworthy, personalised understanding.

## Why Existing Tools Are Not Enough

Existing tools solve only part of the problem.

- Literature databases store papers but not consolidated knowledge.
- AI assistants answer questions but rarely maintain long-term memory.
- Note-taking applications preserve information but do not evaluate evidence.
- Reference managers organise publications but do not synthesise evolving scientific consensus.

There is currently no system that continuously transforms scientific literature into a trustworthy, living knowledge base tailored to an individual patient profile.

## Design Goals

The system should:

- Continuously monitor new scientific evidence.
- Preserve historical knowledge.
- Track changes in scientific consensus.
- Separate objective evidence from personal interpretation.
- Generate personalised insights without modifying scientific facts.
- Support informed conversations with healthcare professionals.
